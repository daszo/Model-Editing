Lever 1 — launch in bf16

The repo's Accelerator() defaults to fp32, so a bare python GR/training.py quietly costs 2×. Always accelerate launch --mixed_precision bf16.

Lever 2 — the card throttles ~4×

This 3090 boosts to 1950 MHz cold, then collapses to ~500 MHz and stays there. Cooling-limited, not power-limited. Fixing it could halve the run — a bigger win than switching models.

01The throttling story

The single most important thing about this hardware. My first reading looked great — 0.485 s/step, a two-day run. It was a cold-GPU mirage. Here is the SM clock through one sustained run, sampled once a second:

Why it matters: a multi-day run is permanently heat-soaked, so the sustained ~1.7 s/step — not the cold 0.485 — is the number to plan around. Before the real run, try a higher fan curve, better case airflow, a re-paste, or a lower stable power cap (nvidia-smi -pl 250), then re-measure with a 2-minute benchmark. Every 2× of clock recovered is ~2× off the week.

03Precision & optimizer

t5-large, batch 128, sustained. bf16 + AdamW is the pick: it fits at 19.3 G and is fastest. Adafactor is a memory lever, not a speed one; fp32 just doubles the wall-clock.

04The path I'd take

  1. Fix cooling, then re-measure

    Biggest lever, zero accuracy cost. Confirm s/step drops before committing a week.

    ./.venv/bin/python notes/benchmarks/bench_train.py --model_name t5-large

  2. Always launch in bf16

    accelerate launch --mixed_precision bf16 --num_processes 1 \ GR/training.py --batch_size 128

  3. Add resume-to-checkpoint before starting

    An 8-day run will be interrupted — you already force-rebooted once. The script has no resume today; a crash loses everything since the last 5-epoch save. ~20 lines.

  4. Run a t5-base pilot end-to-end (2.5 d)

    Won't match the paper's Hits@k, but it exercises the whole chain — training → get_knowns → DOME editing → eval — and surfaces breakage on day 2, not day 7.

  5. Commit the faithful t5-large run

    ~7.5 d at 35 epochs with current cooling; less if step 1 paid off.
